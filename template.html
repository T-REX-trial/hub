What happens at each visit

Assessments per follow-up visit, from the protocol schedule. Hover over or tap a visit in the list or calendar above to see the same detail for one participant.

The post-CRT visit is one month after the last RT fraction. Colonoscopy is timed from local excision (12 months), then after 3 years, or after 1 year if an advanced adenoma is found. Windows: ±2 weeks up to month 9, ±1 month from month 12.
